To drop your half marathon time from 1:25 to sub-1:20, you'll need a well-structured training plan that focuses on speed, endurance, and strength. Here's a 12-week plan based on your current fitness, with some added volume and intensity to help you hit your goal:

Weekly Overview:
Mileage: 45-55 miles per week
Key workouts: 1 tempo run, 1 long run, 1 interval workout
Rest days: 1-2 per week (optional cross-training)
Training Paces:
Goal half marathon pace (HMP): ~6:05/mile
Threshold pace (T): ~6:15-6:25/mile
Interval pace (I): ~5:45-5:55/mile
Easy run pace (E): 7:15-8:00/mile
Week 1-4: Base Building + Threshold Focus
Monday: Easy Run
5-7 miles at an easy pace (7:30-8:00/mile)
Tuesday: Interval Workout
4 x 1 mile at threshold pace (6:15-6:25/mile) with 1-minute standing rest
Cool down 1-2 miles
Wednesday: Medium Long Run
8-10 miles at an easy pace
Thursday: Tempo Run
4 miles at half marathon pace (6:05/mile) + warm-up/cool-down
Friday: Rest or Cross-training
Optional light swimming, cycling, or strength training
Saturday: Long Run
12-14 miles at an easy pace (7:15-7:45/mile)
Sunday: Recovery Run
4-6 miles at a very easy pace (7:45-8:15/mile)
Week 5-8: Speed and Strength Development
Monday: Easy Run
5-6 miles easy + strides
Tuesday: Interval Workout
5 x 800m at interval pace (5:45-5:55/mile) with 90 seconds rest
Cool down 1-2 miles
Wednesday: Medium Long Run
9-11 miles at an easy pace
Thursday: Tempo + Speed Work
3 miles at threshold pace (6:15-6:25/mile), 4 x 200m at mile pace
Cool down
Friday: Rest or Cross-training
Saturday: Long Run with Speed
14-16 miles, last 3-4 miles at half marathon pace (6:05/mile)
Sunday: Recovery Run
5-7 miles at an easy pace
Week 9-12: Race-Specific Preparation
Monday: Easy Run
5-6 miles + strides
Tuesday: VO2 Max Workout
3 x 1.5 miles at 10k pace (5:50-6:00/mile) with 3 minutes rest
Cool down
Wednesday: Medium Long Run
9-12 miles at an easy pace
Thursday: Tempo Run
5-6 miles at goal half marathon pace (6:05/mile) + warm-up/cool-down
Friday: Rest or Cross-training
Saturday: Long Run with Race Simulation
16 miles, last 4-5 miles at goal half marathon pace
Sunday: Recovery Run
5-6 miles easy
Taper (Final 2 weeks):
Gradually reduce mileage to 30-35 miles per week
Maintain intensity with shorter tempo runs and intervals
Long run reduced to 10-12 miles
Stay fresh and avoid overtraining
Key Focus Areas:
Tempo runs will improve your lactate threshold, crucial for holding your goal pace.
Intervals will boost your VO2 max and speed, making race pace feel easier.
Long runs with goal pace segments will help your body adapt to running fast over distance.
This plan will help you make the leap to a sub-1:20 half marathon! How does it look for you?
